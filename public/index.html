Questions

Good to know.

Can’t find your answer? Send us a message.

What do you sell?

Classic, affordable clothing and sunglasses — our signature 100% cotton joggers, plus wide-leg pants, shorts, tops and shades. Everything is available retail and wholesale.

How does wholesale pricing work?

Once you add the minimum wholesale quantity of a style to your bag (mix any colours and sizes), the wholesale price is applied automatically. For bulk or custom orders, send a wholesale request and we’ll quote you directly.

Where do you deliver?

Everywhere in Nigeria. We’re in Lagos and Ibadan, so we offer doorstep delivery in both cities, and we ship to all 36 states and the FCT. We’ll confirm your delivery timeline when we confirm your order.

How do I pay?

After you place your order you’ll see our payment details, and we’ll also reach out to confirm your order and payment before dispatch.

How do I choose my size?

Most styles come in S to XXL (with age sizes for kids). If you’re between sizes or unsure, message us on WhatsApp with your waist and height and we’ll recommend the best fit.

Can I track my order?

Yes — tap Track and enter your order number and the phone number you used at checkout.
